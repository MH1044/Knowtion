## Licence policy

| Licence | Status |
|---|---|
| MIT, BSD-2, BSD-3, ISC, Apache-2.0, CC0, Unlicense, public domain | Allowed |
| MPL-2.0 | Allowed as an unmodified dependency; modifications must be published |
| GPL, AGPL, SSPL, BUSL, source-available | **Build fails** |

CI enforces this from the first commit. See CONTRIBUTING.md for the clean-room rule
covering prior art we may read but not copy.
