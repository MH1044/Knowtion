Pages inside pages, and links between them: the start of building a wiki the way you do
in Notion. Still pre-alpha: keep a copy of anything you would be upset to lose.

## Pages and navigation

- **Pages inside pages.** `/page` in the `/` menu makes a page inside the one you are on
  and opens it, with the caret in its title. A page lists the pages inside it after its
  text, each a click away, with "Add a page inside" at the end. A breadcrumb above the
  title links every page above this one.
- **Mention another page with `@`.** Typing `@` and part of a title offers the matching
  pages. A mention shows the page's icon and title, opens the page when clicked, and
  follows a rename or a new icon. A mention of a deleted page, or of one this device does
  not have yet, says "Page not found" and stays as it is.
- **Jump to any page with Ctrl+P.** Quick Find opens over the page from anywhere, listing
  the pages you opened last until you type, then the pages whose title or text matches,
  title matches first, each with its icon and the pages above it. Up, Down and Enter open
  one; Escape puts you back where you were. Ctrl+K opens it too, except in the text, where
  it still makes a link. **Ctrl+N makes a new page**, as the New page button does.
- **Go back and forward through the pages you opened** with Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+], the arrows
  at the top left of the page, or a mouse's back and forward buttons, however each page
  was opened. A page moved to the trash is stepped over. **Ctrl+\ hides the sidebar**, as
  does the « beside New page, and the page takes the whole window; the » at the top left
  or Ctrl+\ again brings it back, and Knowtion remembers which you chose.
- **Knowtion opens where you left it.** Launching it opens the page you had open when you
  closed it, a database row included, or the page you opened before that if it has since
  gone to the trash. **The window is named after the open page**, such as
  "Meeting notes - Knowtion", so the taskbar and Alt+Tab say which page it is, and it
  follows a rename as you type.

## Writing

- **Type a date in words after `@`.** `@Oct 3`, `@3 October 2027`, `@10/3`,
  `@next friday`, `@last friday`, `@in 2 weeks` and the like offer that date as you type,
  and Enter puts it in the line. Numbers are read the way your computer writes dates, so
  `3/10` is 3 October where the day comes first.
- **The ⋮⋮ block menu works from the keyboard.** Up and Down move through it, Enter
  chooses, and Escape closes it with the caret back in the text. "Turn into" opens the
  list of block types beside the menu, as in Notion.
- **Right-click in a page for a menu.** It has Cut, Copy and Paste, then Duplicate, Delete
  and "Turn into" for the block you clicked, so a line can become a heading from there.
  Right-clicking a page mention or a date opens the menu rather than the page or the
  calendar.
- **Turn selected text into any block type.** The toolbar over selected text now names the
  block it is in, such as "Bulleted list", and turns every line you selected into text, a
  heading, a list, a to-do, a toggle, a callout, a quote or code. Lines turned into a
  numbered list become one list numbered 1, 2, 3, and the text stays selected, so Bold
  still applies to it.
- **Notion's block keys.** Ctrl+D puts a copy of the line, or of the lines you selected,
  just below. Ctrl+Shift+Up and Down move them one place, out of a list or a toggle at its
  ends. Ctrl+Alt+0 to 8 make the line text, a heading 1 to 3, a to-do, a bulleted or
  numbered list, a toggle or code, from inside a list or a to-do too, and Ctrl+Shift+0 to
  8 do the same, as in Notion on Windows. The caret stays where it was.
- **Esc selects the block the caret is in**, tinted as in Notion: a list item, or a whole
  toggle from its first line. Up and Down then select the block above or below, Enter
  puts the caret back at the end of its text, and Backspace or Delete removes it. Ctrl+D,
  Ctrl+Shift+Up and Down and Ctrl+Alt+0 to 8 act on it and leave it selected, Ctrl+C
  copies it whole, and a stray letter typed does not replace it; text from a Japanese,
  Chinese or Korean input method goes in at the end of its text.

## Databases

- **Dates open the same calendar everywhere.** A date cell, a date in a row opened as a
  page, and a filter's "Exact date…" open the calendar a date in the text opens, on the
  date's month, with Today and Clear. Clear empties the cell, or leaves the filter clause
  waiting for a date, so it stops filtering.
- **Board cards show tags in their colours.** A select or multi-select value on a card is
  drawn as the same coloured tag the table shows, rather than as plain text, so a status
  or a label reads at a glance on either view.

## Fixes

## Pages written by this version

Page mentions are a new kind of content. 0.4.0, 0.4.1 and 0.4.2 open a page that has one
read-only, with a note, rather than losing it. 0.3.1 and earlier cannot tell, and may
drop them, so do not open such pages in those versions.

## Install (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

Download **Knowtion-Setup-0.5.0.exe** below and run it. **Windows will show a blue
"Windows protected your PC" box.** This build is not code-signed, so SmartScreen has no
publisher to vouch for it. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Installing over 0.4.x
keeps your data.

## Known limits

- Windows only, and unsigned.
- No images or attachments yet.
- A page inside a page is listed after the text; it cannot sit in the middle of it yet.
- `@` dates have no times, end dates or reminders yet.
- One device per workspace for now.
