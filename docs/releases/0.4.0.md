The editor finally shows you what it can do: type / for any block, select text for a
formatting bar, and grab the handle beside a block to move or change it. Plus toggles,
callouts and dates with a calendar. Still pre-alpha: keep a copy of anything you would be
upset to lose.

## An editor you can use without knowing the shortcuts

Until now every heading, list and quote was hidden behind a keyboard shortcut or a
markdown prefix, and nothing on screen said so. In practice that made the app feel like
it could only write plain text.

- **Type `/`** at the start of a line, or after a space, for a menu of every block. Keep
  typing to narrow it (`/head`, `/list`), then press Enter or click. Each entry shows its
  markdown shortcut, so next time you can skip the menu.
- **Select text** and a bar appears with bold, italic, strikethrough, code and link, plus
  a menu to turn the line into a heading.
- **Hover a block** and a `+` and a grip appear beside it. `+` adds a line below with the
  menu open. Drag the grip to move the block, with a line showing where it will land, or
  click it to duplicate, delete, or turn the block into something else.

## Toggles, callouts and dates

- **Toggle lists** fold everything under their first line behind an arrow.
- **Callouts** set text apart in a box with an icon. Click the icon to change it.
- **Dates**: type `@` for Today, Tomorrow, Yesterday or Next week. The date becomes a chip
  that reads "Tomorrow" or "Oct 15". Click it for a calendar to pick any other day.
  Searching for a date finds the pages it appears in.

Importing from Notion now brings toggles and callouts across as toggles and callouts,
rather than flattening them into text and quotes.

## Safer with more than one version around

A page written by a newer version of Knowtion now opens read-only in an older one, with
a note saying so. Before this, an older version could silently delete the parts it did
not understand just by opening the page. This version is the first with that protection.
So **do not open pages that use toggles, callouts or dates in 0.3.1 or earlier.** Those
builds cannot tell that they don't understand them.

## Fixes

- Choosing a sync folder no longer leaves the app writing unencrypted changes into it
  until the next restart.
- Choosing a folder that already holds another device's workspace is refused for now,
  rather than joining in a way that has never worked. Joining a workspace from a second
  device is the next thing on the devices list in docs/ROADMAP.md.
- A second space, or a space at the end of a line, no longer disappears on screen.

## Install (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

Download **Knowtion-Setup-0.4.0.exe** below and run it. **Windows will show a blue
"Windows protected your PC" box.** This build is not code-signed, so SmartScreen has no
publisher to vouch for it. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

It installs for your user only: no administrator password, nothing written outside your
profile. Installing over 0.3.1 keeps your data.
